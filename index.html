03

MULTI-MODEL CODING · ROUTING · OBSERVABILITY

My Coding
Harness

Models fail differently. Why send every task to the same one—or discover failure after the work is over?

I made a measured router and live dashboard around local, free, and frontier models. Workers can run in parallel; real task results rank them; dead streams and rate limits trigger bounded failover; privacy rules constrain where sensitive code can go.

04

MARKET RESEARCH · TEMPORAL DATA · RISK GATES

Guarded Market
Automation

Financial automation is easy to demo and hard to make honest.

I built research and paper-trading paths where every claim must have been knowable at decision time. Risk can veto. Approvals bind to exact proposals. Fresh preflight runs before paper execution, and fills reconcile into a durable ledger.

No profitability claims. No autonomous live-money authority. Refusing weak evidence is a feature.
